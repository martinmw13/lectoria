# Art sources

- Chopin portrait, the source for the skins' Chopin art below: "Frederic Chopin photo.jpeg", daguerreotype by Louis-Auguste Bisson, 1849. Public domain (Wikimedia Commons, license field "Public domain"; author died 1876). https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Frederic_Chopin_photo.jpeg .
- panels/cyclone-doorway.jpg, panels/cyclone-house.jpg: images made by Meta's image model during the Lectoria image spike (Denslow-style prompts), recompressed to JPEG. Placeholder panel art for chapter 1 until panels are drawn. panels/cyclone-house.webp is the house image converted to WebP (quality 75) with sharp, same pixels, served on the home page.
- covers/{book}-{engraving|watercolor|ink}.jpg (480×640) and -sm.jpg (150×200) for alice, frankenstein, pride, sherlock, dracula, peterpan, treasure, jungle, carol: placeholder library covers made for the home-v4 prototype with Grok Bot's image tool. The engraving cover came first; the watercolor and ink covers use it as reference. To be replaced by Meta-made covers. The books are public domain.
- skins/pencil/rain0-3.png, skins/pencil/pic.png, skins/pencil/paper.jpg: images made by Meta's image model for the approved pencil "Study" skin prototype, downscaled with Pillow (icons to 128 px, paper to 1400x788 JPEG). The Orchestra, Piano and Read aloud drawings (skins/pencil/orchestra.png, piano.png, narrate.png) come from the same prototype, cropped and downscaled to 128 px; skins/pencil/chopin.png is the public-domain Bisson daguerreotype above, greyed and cut into a ringed circle with Pillow.
- skins/chihiro/rain0-3.png, skins/chihiro/pic.png, skins/chihiro/sky-lantern.jpg: images made by Meta's image model for the approved chihiro "Lantern" skin prototype, downscaled with Pillow (icons to 128 px, sky to 1800x323 JPEG).
- skins/engraving/{orchestra,piano,chopin,narrate}.svg: the engraved line art from the approved engraving skin prototype (prototipos/decidido/skins, src icons.js and defs.svg), exported as standalone SVGs on 2026-10-28. Drawn for Lectoria during the hackathon. The Chopin plate is an engraved trace of the Bisson daguerreotype above (public domain).
- skins/watercolor/{orchestra,piano,narrate}.svg: the painted art from the approved watercolor skin prototype (prototipos/decidido/skins, src icons.js and defs.svg), exported as standalone SVGs on 2026-10-28. Drawn for Lectoria during the hackathon.
- skins/ink/{orchestra,piano,chopin,narrate}.svg: the pen-drawn art from the approved ink skin prototype (prototipos/decidido/skins, src icons.js and defs.svg), exported as standalone SVGs on 2026-10-28. Drawn for Lectoria during the hackathon; the Chopin portrait inside chopin.svg is the Bisson daguerreotype above (public domain).
- skins/watercolor/chopin.png: a watercolor portrait of Chopin generated from a text prompt with Meta's Muse Image (`muse-image-1.0`), cut into a ringed circle and downscaled to 128 px with Pillow.
- ../covers/v1/<skin>/<book>.webp (and the small copies in sm/): shelf covers generated with Meta's Muse Image (`muse-image-1.0`) by scripts/pregen-covers.ts, one per skin and shelf book, resized with sharp.
- ../portraits/oz.webp, oz-uncle-henry.webp, oz-aunt-em.webp, oz-toto.webp, their @2x copies and the same files under ../portraits/skins/<skin>/: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz character portraits (Dorothy, Uncle Henry, Aunt Em and Toto), generated with Meta's Muse Image (`muse-image-1.0`) and downscaled to 256 and 512 px with Pillow.
